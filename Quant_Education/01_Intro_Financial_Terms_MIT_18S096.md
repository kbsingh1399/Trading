# MIT 18.S096: Lecture 1 - Introduction, Financial Terms and Concepts

## Comprehensive Educational Notes & Lecture Lexicon

### Core Themes Covered
- Financial Asset Taxonomy: Equities, Fixed Income, Foreign Exchange (FX), and Commodities.
- Market Intermediation & Infrastructure: Central Clearing Counterparties (CCPs), Limit Order Books (LOB), and Prime Brokerage.
- Derivative Architecture & Valuation: Futures, Forwards, Options, Swaps, Put-Call Parity, and Cash-and-Carry Arbitrage.
- Market Microstructure: Short Selling, Borrow Rates, Locate Fees, Short Rebates, and Short Squeeze Dynamics.
- Risk Taxonomy: Market, Credit, Operational, Model, and Liquidity Risk (Exogenous vs Endogenous).

### 14-Term Technical Lexicon
1. **Cross-Currency Basis Swap**: Floating-for-floating currency swap measuring systemic offshore USD funding stress.
2. **Samurai / Yankee Bonds**: Foreign debt issued in local currency jurisdictions to diversify institutional funding pools.
3. **Short Rebate & Locate Fee**: Net yield on short sale cash collateral after deducting hard-to-borrow prime broker fees.
4. **Delta Neutrality**: Portfolio insulation where first-order price sensitivity ∂Π/∂S = 0.
5. **Cash-and-Carry Arbitrage**: Simultaneous spot purchase and forward sale locking in the riskless net carry.
6. **Convenience Yield**: Embedded dividend yield of holding physical commodity inventories during immediate scarcity.
7. **Contango vs Backwardation**: Positive carry forward premium vs spot scarcity discount driving ETF roll yields.
8. **Bid-Ask Spread & Slippage**: Market maker adverse selection premium and execution friction.
9. **Variation vs Initial Margin**: Upfront safety cushion (IM) vs daily mark-to-market cash settlement (VM).
10. **Haircut & Repurchase Agreements (Repo)**: Collateralized money-market financing with asset haircuts.
11. **Law of One Price**: Foundation of relative value arbitrage dictating identical prices for identical cash flows.
12. **Order Book Depth & Market Impact**: Microstructure liquidity and execution price degradation.
13. **Fixed-for-Floating IRS**: Liability transformation swaps exchanging floating SOFR for fixed coupons.
14. **Exogenous vs Endogenous Liquidity Risk**: Market-wide spread widening vs trader-induced fire-sale price depression.
