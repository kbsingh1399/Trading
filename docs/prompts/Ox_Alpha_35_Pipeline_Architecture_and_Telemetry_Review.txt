[THE EMPIRICAL PROBLEM: LIVE FORWARD TELEMETRY & SPREAD TRAP]
====================================================================================================
During live forward dry-run testing on MetaTrader 5 (Blueberry Markets demo feed), the engine experienced premature stop-outs where trades exited at bar count 0 (immediate execution stop-out):
- Root Cause Analysis:
  In tight Asian or European pre-market sessions, the Opening Range (first 15m or 30m bar) was frequently narrow (e.g. 0.8 to 2.5 pips on FX pairs, or 0.8 to 1.5 points on indices).
  When ORB breakout signals fired, the raw structural stop loss was placed just outside the opening range extreme.
  Consequently, the raw distance from entry to stop loss was narrower than the broker spread (or barely 1x spread).
  The instant a market order crossed the spread, the trade was liquidated immediately.

[THE SOLUTION: OPTION C MICROSTRUCTURE HARDENING]
====================================================================================================
Our team designed and integrated Option C Microstructure Hardening into Engine/forex_engine.py, Engine/ORB_CRT_ForexCFD_Strategy.py, and Engine/FVG_ML_ForexCFD_Strategy.py:

1. Dynamic Spread-to-ATR Ratio Quarantine:
   - Evaluates: spread_atr_ratio = current_spread / atr_14
   - Invariant: If spread_atr_ratio > 0.12 (Spread consumes > 12% of 15m ATR), the asset is strictly quarantined from new order generation.
   - Result: Automatically quenches toxic, wide-spread assets during high-spread rollover hours (e.g. EURHUF, EURSEK, GAS, USDHKD, NICKEL).

2. Triple-Floor Adaptive Stop Loss Expansion:
   - Raw Stop: Distance from entry to local swing extreme.
   - Spread Floor: 3.5 * current_spread (Guarantees stop is at least 350% of broker spread).
   - ATR Floor: 1.5 * atr_14 (Guarantees trade withstands normal 15m bar noise).
   - Mathematical Formulation:
     adaptive_sl_dist = max(raw_sl_dist, 3.5 * current_spread, 1.5 * atr_14)
   - Structural Swing Fallback: For index CFDs (GER40, AU200, FR40), scans the 20-bar lowest low (for longs) or 20-bar highest high (for shorts).

3. Three-Phase Microstructure Exit Ratchet:
   - Phase 0 (Break-Even Lock): When unrealized gain reaches +0.80R, advance stop loss to Entry + 0.15R (securing fees + positive slippage clearance).
   - Phase 1 (Profit Lock): When unrealized gain reaches +1.50R, advance stop loss to Entry + 0.80R.
   - Target Exit: Take profit at +2.50R (purged unachievable 4R/5R moonshot targets).
   - Time Decay Exit: If unrealized gain remains below +0.20R after 24 bars (6 hours), exit at market.

4. Multi-Tier Drawdown Governor:
   - Peak Capital Tracking: High-water mark updated bar-by-bar.
   - Hard Freeze Stop: If current drawdown >= 225.00 USD (4.50% of 5,000.00 USD capital), risk budget = 0.00 USD (all trading halts immediately).
   - Drawdown Defense Mode: If current drawdown >= 100.00 USD (2.00%), risk budget drops from 50.00 USD down to 15.00 USD.
   - House Money Expansion: If net cumulative profit >= +50.00 USD, risk budget steps up to 75.00 USD.
   - Base Risk Budget: 50.00 USD (1.00% of base capital).
