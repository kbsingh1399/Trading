  `low[t-1] < low[t-2]` AND `low[t] > low[t-1]` (Zero lookahead; strictly backward-looking).
- **Liquidation Capitulation Spike:**
  Rolling 100-bar Z-score of forced long liquidations:

  `Delta = taker_buy_quote_volume - (quote_volume - taker_buy_quote_volume)`
