### Detailed Resolution of Ox_Alpha_41 Audit Blockers:

1. **Dry-Run Execution Barrier & State Isolation (Section 2 Blocker)**:
   - In `Engine/live/order_manager.py`:
     * Changed `OrderManager.__init__` default to fail-closed paper mode: `def __init__(self, connection=None, dry_run: bool = True, state_file=None):`.
     * State files are completely decoupled: `DRY_RUN_STATE_FILE = Path(__file__).resolve().parent / "dry_run_state.json"` for paper mode vs `LIVE_STATE_FILE = Path(__file__).resolve().parent / "live_state.json"` for live trading.
     * In `Engine/live/run_forex_dry_run.py`: Explicitly wired `order_mgr = OrderManager(mt5_conn, dry_run=is_dry_run)`.
     * In `OrderManager.manage_open_trades()`: In dry-run mode, order management executes offline paper simulation without broker reconciliation interference or deleting simulated tickets.
     * Zero `mt5.order_send` calls can occur under `--dry-run`.

2. **Mark-to-Market Equity & Risk Governor Wiring (Section 3 Blocker)**:
   - In `Engine/research/forward_test_harness.py`:
     * In `run_forward_test()`, before running the auction or admitting candidates, the loop queries live ticks for all active `open_trades` to collect side-specific marks (bid for BUY, ask for SELL).
     * Computes `marked_eq = state.get_marked_equity(current_prices)`.
     * Hard DD stop condition checks `state.max_dd_pct > HARD_DD_STOP_PCT` against marked equity.
     * When candidates are admitted by the auction, candidate risk is sized explicitly via `assigned_risk = state.get_current_risk_usd(marked_equity=marked_eq)` and recorded in `state.open_trades[sig.symbol]['risk_usd']`.
     * If an open position suffers an unrealized drawdown >= 2.0%, candidate risk immediately throttles from 50.00 USD down to 15.00 USD defense risk.

3. **None-Safe Broker Query Preservation in Management (Section 4 Blocker)**:
   - In `Engine/live/order_manager.py`:
     * Updated `manage_open_trades()`:
       ```python
       position = mt5.positions_get(ticket=ticket)
       if position is None:
           log.error(f"[MANAGE ERROR] mt5.positions_get(ticket={ticket}) returned None. Preserving position.")
           continue
       if len(position) == 0:
           # Position confirmed closed by broker
           del self.open_trades[ticket]
           self.save_state()
           continue
       ```
     * Prevents transient broker query failures from deleting open positions from local state.

4. **Risk Sizing Invariant & Lot Step Flooring (Section 5 Blocker)**:
   - In `Engine/live/order_manager.py`:
     * Replaced nearest-step rounding with broker step flooring:
       ```python
       raw_lots = risk_usd / loss_per_lot
       lots = math.floor((raw_lots / step) + 1e-9) * step
       while lots > 0 and (lots * loss_per_lot) > (risk_usd + 1e-6):
           lots = round(lots - step, 8)
       ```
     * Under the hypothetical test case (budget 16.00 USD, loss per lot 1,000.00 USD, raw lots 0.016, step 0.01), `lots` is strictly floored to 0.01 lots (10.00 USD loss), never rounding up to 0.02 lots (20.00 USD loss).
     * Explicit volume supplied in `place_market_order()` is validated and clamped against maximum allowable risk lots (`max_allowed_lots`).
     * Real broker fills: Upon order execution, `result.price` and `result.volume` are recorded in `open_trades` rather than requested quote values.

5. **Lifetime Metrics Ledger Across Process Restarts (Section 9 Blocker)**:
   - In `Engine/research/forward_test_harness.py`:
     * `ForwardState` now persists cumulative counters: `total_completed_trades`, `total_wins`, and `total_losses`.
     * Dynamically updates `self.lifetime_max_dd_ratio` and `self.lifetime_max_dd_usd` on every trade closure and MTM calculation.
     * Properties `completed_trades`, `win_rate`, `max_dd_pct`, and `calmar_live` utilize the persistent lifetime counters, eliminating metric distortion when a truncated 500-trade history slice is reloaded.

6. **Quarantine Hysteresis Hardening & Cross-Module Parity (Sections 6 & 7)**:
   - Synchronized `StatefulInferenceEngine` in `Engine/forex_engine.py` with `Engine/live/inference_engine.py`:
     * Added `buffer_d1` to support daily boundary features.
     * `check_quarantine()` now checks `None`, `NaN`, `Inf`, and non-positive spread/ATR, resetting `self.is_quarantined = True` and `self.consecutive_safe_bars = 0`.
     * Added `bar_time` parameter requiring 2 unique completed bars before clearing quarantine.
     * In `Engine/live/run_forex_dry_run.py`, passed `completed_bar_time` to `check_quarantine(..., bar_time=completed_bar_time)`.
