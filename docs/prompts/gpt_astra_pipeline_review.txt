2. **Feature Engineering (13 Canonical Stationary Features):**
   - ICT Microstructure: Bullish & Bearish Fair Value Gap (FVG) exact wick magnitudes.
   - Liquidity Sweeps: Previous Day High (PDH) and Previous Day Low (PDL) sweeps.
   - Multi-Timeframe Alignment: 4H EMA 200 momentum slope (`ema_200_4h[t] - ema_200_4h[t-5]`).
   - Technical Regimes: Wilder's RSI(14), 20-period typical price percentage distance (VWAP proxy), EMA 50/200 percentage distances, 3-hour EMA 200 slope, Volatility(20), Rate of Change ROC(20), and ATR(14).
   - Session Kill Zones: London Open (07:00–10:00 UTC) and New York Open (12:00–15:00 UTC).

3. **Machine Learning Model & Labeling:**
   - Architecture: XGBoost Classifier (`max_depth=4`, `learning_rate=0.05`, `reg_alpha=1.0`, `reg_lambda=3.0`, `subsample=0.8`, `colsample_bytree=0.8`, `scale_pos_weight=9.43`).
   - Labeling Protocol: Triple-Barrier Method (+4.0R Take Profit vs -1.0R Stop Loss, max holding window of 96 bars / 24 hours).
   - Walk-Forward Validation: Evaluated across 20 non-overlapping random 1-month Out-Of-Sample (OOS) windows from 2024 to 2026. All 20 passed with Net PnL > $500/mo (>+10.0% net on $5,000 capital), Max DD < 4.5%, and Win Rate > 40.0%.

4. **Microstructure Exit Ratchets (Anti-Retracement Geometry):**
   - High-frequency piecewise trailing stop to lock in EV and eliminate trade round-trips:
     * Price >= +1.0R -> Move Stop Loss to Entry +0.15R (Breakeven + broker commission/friction)
     * Price >= +1.5R -> Move Stop Loss to Entry +1.0R
     * Price >= +2.0R -> Move Stop Loss to Entry +1.8R (0.2R tight trailing margin)
     * Price >= +2.5R -> Move Stop Loss to Entry +2.3R
     * Price >= +3.0R -> Move Stop Loss to Entry +2.8R
     * Price >= +3.5R -> Move Stop Loss to Entry +3.3R
     * Take Profit: Exit at +4.0R
     * Time Decay Exit: Market close if trade fails to gain +0.20R within 24 bars (6 hours).

5. **Live Dry-Run Telemetry Engine:**
   - Automated Pre-Flight Sync: At startup, scans parquets in `Forex_Backtesting_Data/`, connects to MT5 broker, pulls any missing 15m closed candles, and appends them to parquet files before launching.
   - Dual-Timeframe State Buffer: Maintains 250 bars of 15m and 4H in memory to eliminate cold-start indicator distortion.
   - Live Telemetry Dashboard: Continuously updates Bid/Ask, floating spreads, RSI, VWAP dist, EMAs, 4H trends, and model prediction probability every 1.5 seconds.
   - Safety Lockout: `DRY_RUN = True` hardcoded. Simulates signal logs without placing market orders.
