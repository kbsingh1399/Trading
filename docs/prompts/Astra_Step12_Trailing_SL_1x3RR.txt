## Fixes Already Applied Locally

We identified and patched 3 issues in simulate_orb_trades() before this run:

FIX 1 — RR was 1:2.5, not 1:3:
  - tp = entry + 2.5 * r_val → corrected to tp = entry + 3.0 * r_val (both long and short)
  - outcome hardcode was 2.5 → corrected to 3.0
  - outcome cap was min(2.5, ...) → corrected to min(3.0, ...)

FIX 2 — Phase 0 BE lock was friction-blind:
  - current_sl = entry + 0.15 * r_val → corrected to entry + 0.35 * r_val
  - At 41 bps round-trip friction, 0.15R → net -0.19R guaranteed. 0.35R clears it with 0.01R margin.

FIX 3 — Flat 8 bps friction was dimensionally wrong:
  - Was: outcome_r -= 0.08 (treats 8 bps as a fixed R deduction regardless of r_val vs notional)
  - Now: proper institutional fee model, dynamically computed in R units:
    Entry: 8 bps taker + 10 bps slippage = 18 bps
    Exit TP/timeout: 8 bps taker + 10 bps slippage = 18 bps
    Exit stop-hit: 8 bps taker + 15 bps stop slippage = 23 bps
    entry_friction_r = (entry * 0.0018) / r_val
    exit_friction_r = (entry * 0.0023) / r_val  [stop] or (entry * 0.0018) / r_val [normal]
    outcome_r -= (entry_friction_r + exit_friction_r)
