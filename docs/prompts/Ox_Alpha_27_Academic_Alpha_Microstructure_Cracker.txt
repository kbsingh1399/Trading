   - Finding: Based on 1-second Binance Futures tick data (2022-2025), order flow imbalance exhibits a concave, saturating predictive shape best modeled by tanh(scale * TFI). Furthermore, price deviations from VWAP exhibit strong asymmetric mean reversion, and extreme spread regimes degrade directional predictability.
